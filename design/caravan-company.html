Mojave Caravan Company

You run a travelling merchant company from a terminal. Farms, the spring and the quarry make raw goods. Your workshops turn them into things people buy, and your caravans carry them to the towns and the NCR. The Crimson Caravan is your rival and your lender. The NCR licenses you, taxes you, and raids you if you smuggle.

The loop

Decided

Every part of the company sits on one loop. You steer it only from the terminal. Everything else in the Mojave pushes on one link of it.

How the company grows

Proposed

Five tiers. Each one shows up in the world and adds a new kind of thing to do, not just bigger numbers. Pick a tier to see what you would have and where. The first version covers Goodsprings, Primm, Sloan and Mojave Outpost Decided

    Your adventuring feeds the company

    • Escort a run yourself for a bonus.
    • Clear a gang off a route and that road stays safer.
    • Close a contract in person.
    • Recruit a resident.

    Your quest choices move the market

    • Primm without a sheriff: risky road, high demand for ammo.
    • Powder Gangers driven off: safer Goodsprings.
    • Quarry Junction cleared: Sloan's stone flows.

    Nothing stays easy

    • When a route gets profitable, the Crimson Caravan moves in.
    • New goods and routes open with each tier, so early income stops being the point.
    • Managers take a cut, so automation is never free.

    How you can lose

    Proposed

    Two ways the company can die. Both are visible long before they happen, with a way out at every step. Everything else costs pieces, not the company.

    Money: the Crimson Caravan holds your debt

    You start on a loan from Alice McLafferty. In the vanilla story she bought out Cass's company after its caravans were wrecked. The weekly payment and a countdown sit on the terminal's front page.

    1. 1
      A payment is missed

      A warning and a late fee.

      Way out: pay late, sell stock.

    2. 2
      Another is missed

      Alice seizes a warehouse or a caravan.

      Way out: sell a warehouse, take an advance on an NCR contract, or pay with smuggling money.

    3. 3
      A third, or three weeks in the red

      Takeover. Your office becomes a Crimson Caravan branch, your sign comes down, and your staff wear her colours.

    Paying the loan off is a milestone of its own. After that, the same steps run on wages, rent and tax.

    Heat: the NCR shuts you down

    Only on the dodgy side. Heat comes from how much contraband you move, what kind, and how much the NCR dislikes you.

    1. 1
      Heat rises

      Shown on the hidden ledger.

      Lower it: bribes, a fixer, hidden warehouses, lying low.

    2. 2
      Tip-offs

      Contacts warn you a raid is coming.

      Way out: move the stock, pay someone, go quiet.

    3. 3
      Raid

      At HQ it's real: bribe, surrender or fight. Away, it's on paper: cash and contraband seized, staff arrested, office closed for a while.

    4. 4
      Shutdown

      Licence revoked. The company is finished.

    Costs pieces, not the company

    • A caravan lost on the road.
    • A warehouse robbed.
    • Staff who quit when wages are late.
    • Your best traders lured away by Alice.
    • A route closed by danger.

    Your own money

    • Company cash is kept apart from your caps.
    • Paying yourself weakens the company, which makes it a real choice.
    • Whatever you've taken out survives a takeover.
    Open

    After you lose the company, then what? My pick is to start over smaller: you keep the save and found a new company, some former staff come back, and your old office stays a Crimson Caravan branch as a reminder. "Gone for good on that save" fits better as an optional hardcore setting, because players can always reload an earlier save.

    Who's who

    Decided
    You

    The owner. You steer the company from a terminal, and your adventuring opens roads and supply.

    Staff

    Hands, guards and traders. Real town residents you hire. They get better with experience and quit when wages are late.

    Managers

    Route manager, warehouse manager, bookkeeper, and a fixer if you smuggle. Each runs one job and takes a cut; a cheap one plays safe and earns less.

    Skilled hires

    FNV recipes have skill levels, so some steps need the right person: Science 70 for stimpaks, Repair 25 for ammo. Proposed

    Crimson Caravan

    Alice McLafferty's company. Your rival in every town and the holder of your startup loan. Vanilla quests with them stay as they are.

    NCR

    The government. Licence, weekly tax, tolls at Mojave Outpost, supply contracts the Crimson Caravan bids on too, patrols that make roads safer, and raids if you smuggle.

    Powder Gangers

    Based at the NCR prison. Cheap powder for the ammo chain, paid for in heat.

    Raiders and wildlife

    Ambush caravans. Near you it's a real fight; elsewhere the outcome comes from your guards and the route's danger.

    The terminal

    Decided

    The whole company is run from terminals: at your HQ first, and later at each branch office. This is what the front page could show.

    No typing. Terminals take menu choices only, so every action is a pick from a list.

    Menus built by script. JohnnyGuitar NVSE can add, rename, remove and refresh terminal menu items while the game runs.

    Live numbers in the page text. The body text would need a hook on the engine's terminal display function (ComputersMenu::DisplayTerminal, 0x007586E0). Not tested in game yet.

    Requirements. xNVSE and JohnnyGuitar NVSE.

    Where things stand

    Decided
    • A travelling merchant company. You hire managers who run it and traders who work for it.
    • Run like a real company: profit and loss, revenue, warehouses, managers.
    • Managed entirely in a terminal.
    • The Crimson Caravan is the business rival; the NCR is the government.
    • A dodgy smuggling side, with NCR raids on your office as the risk.
    • Routes use production chains, like Transport Fever 2.
    • Agreed: money is worked out once a game day; caravans walk for real when you're near.
    • Agreed: NCR licence, weekly tax, Mojave Outpost tolls, supply contracts, patrols.
    • Agreed: heat meter; raids real at HQ, on paper when you're away.
    • Agreed: first version is Goodsprings, Primm, Sloan and Mojave Outpost.
    Proposed
    • Alice McLafferty's startup loan, and the steps from a missed payment to takeover.
    • NCR shutdown as the second way to lose.
    • Company cash kept apart from your caps.
    • Five tiers, each needing money, town trust and a deed.
    • Market share in each town as the main number that grows.
    • A daily report with one to three decisions; bills weekly.
    • Quest outcomes move prices and danger.
    • The Crimson Caravan moves in on profitable routes.
    • The five chains and where their workshops sit.
