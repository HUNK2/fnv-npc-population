# T1 Goodsprings: what to try

The lived-in Goodsprings is in the core plugin `LivedInMojave.esm`. These are the things only a
person playing can judge. Use a new game or an old save; nothing changes before Doc Mitchell lets
you out of his house.

## A day in town

1. Leave Doc Mitchell's house and stand by the Prospector Saloon. Fourteen new people live in town
   (a fifteenth comes later): the Pruitts, the Okafors, the Harlans, three farmhands in the house
   by the store, Hector the night watchman, and two travellers who stay at the saloon by night.
2. Wait until 6:45 on the Pruitts' porch (house 1, west of the saloon). At 7 Amos walks his wife
   and son out to the new field west of town; the farmhands follow. Does the walk look like a
   family going to work, or like a queue?
3. At noon the families eat at home and the hands at the saloon. At 18 the porches fill up; at
   22 the town goes to bed. Hector walks the road from 21 to 5 and sleeps in a saloon room by day.
4. Does town feel busier but not crowded? Do any two people stand inside each other, block a
   door, or sit on the same chair?

## Trouble

5. Bring a fight into town in the morning (lead geckos or coyotes into the field). Amos, Wes,
   Marisol and Hector fight; everyone else runs home and stays in until about an hour after the
   last shot.
6. Hit one of the residents: Goodsprings likes you less (infamy), as when you hit a vanilla
   settler. Killing one costs more.

## Your choices

7. Win Ghost Town Gunfight for the town: Tea Whitlock arrives the next time the town quest runs
   (10 seconds) and works the field. Residents now greet you with a line about the gunfight.
8. Or side with the Powder Gangers in Run Goodsprings Run: a day later the families and most hands
   have left town. Only Dutchy stays.

## Known gaps

- The watchman lives in the saloon, not the gas station: the gas station's door is too far up the
  hill for an unseen actor to reach the road.
- Rain does not yet send the field workers home, and a high infamy does not yet lock doors or
  keep children in (the model has these; the game does not yet).
- Lines are text only (no voice), shown as a greeting.
