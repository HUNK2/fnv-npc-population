# T1 Goodsprings: proofs (T1.0)

Answers to the questions `docs/towns-design.md` sends to T1.0, with how each was found.

| Question | Answer | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who reads `GoodspringsFaction` (00104C6E) in FalloutNV.esm? | Faction relations of GoodspringsAnimalFaction, GoodspringsFaction and GoodspringsRingoFaction; members DocMitchell, Chet, Easy Pete, Sunny, Trudy, the four GSSettler bases and VCG02GSSettler; one dialogue condition (INFO 0015E42A); scripts: VCG02 adds Ringo (INFO 00105D09); VMS16 `SetAlly GoodspringsFaction PlayerFaction` and `SetEnemy GoodspringsFaction PlayerFaction 1 1`; VMS16b (0015EC5B) removes Sunny, Trudy, Doc Mitchell and Easy Pete when the gangers win; 0010A214 `SetAlly GoodspringsFaction VVictorFaction` | a scan of every CTDA, SCRO, SNAM, XNAM, DATA subrecord and every script text for the FormID and the name |
| Is it safe for the mod's residents to join `GoodspringsFaction`? | Yes. Nothing counts the faction's members; the scripts only set its relation to the player and to Victor, which our residents should follow as the vanilla settlers do. When the gangers win, the one resident who stays (the gang-side hand) is removed from the faction by our town quest, as VMS16b does for the named NPCs | the list above |
| Are the Goodsprings homes' doors locked? | No. Both sides of every door of `GSHouseInterior01` to `04` have no lock data (XLOC absent) and no owner | the same scan, door references with XTEL into or out of the four homes |
| Does a persistent NPC follow its schedule while the player is away? | Yes. With the player in Primm, at 02, 06, 09, 13, 16 and 20 hours all 14 residents are in the cell or within reach of the marker the model gives (97 checks, 0 script errors). Unseen actors move between Goodsprings cells (home to porch, field, saloon) as the hour turns. Two catches: (1) a console clock jump (`set GameHour`) leaves unseen actors behind (a running sleep package keeps its end time; setting an earlier hour turns the clock back), so the check uses the engine's own one-hour Wait step (`fnv_time mode=engine_wait`), as a player's Wait does; (2) the vanilla gas station's outside door is up the hill, about 6,400 units from the watch route, and the watchman never left it, so he rents a saloon room instead (my call) | `tools/ingame_town.py away` |
| Does every resident keep the schedule with the player in town? | Yes. With the player at the saloon and the game clock running in real time (timescale 60, the window in front), at 02, 06, 09, 13, 16 and 20 hours all residents are at the model's place: 97 checks, no script errors. The engine's one-hour wait is not used in town: it put residents where pathing failed | `tools/ingame_town.py here` (log in `tools/.townhere.log`) |
| Why did the child stay in bed? | Her base copied the child template's ACBS flags 0x20B: 0x200 is "no low-level processing", which freezes an unseen actor's packages, and 0x08 is respawn. The child base now uses 0x13 (female, essential, auto-calc) | `fnv_ai_packages`: the sleep package still running at 09:00 after `EvaluatePackage` |
