# M1 One road: what to try

The first milestone is one wagon on one road: Goodsprings to Primm and back. Everything below was
checked by script in a fresh game; these are the things worth seeing with your own eyes.

## Before you start

- Needs xNVSE, JIP LN NVSE and JohnnyGuitar NVSE. Load `LivedInMojave.esm`, then
  `MojaveCaravanCompany.esp`.
- Any save works, new or old. Nothing in a vanilla cell is edited: the company's people and
  terminals are moved in by script the first time the plugin runs.

## 1. Found the company (5 minutes)

1. Go to the Prospector Saloon in Goodsprings. Hollis Webb sits in the corner booth by the
   company terminal. He is Alice McLafferty's agent (Jason Tagg's if Alice is dead).
2. Ask him "You lend money to traders?". Hear the terms: a 3,000 cap loan, 500 of your own caps
   down. If the Powder Gangers run Goodsprings, the pitch changes.
3. Sign. Behind Chet's store a wagon and two pack brahmin appear; a chalkboard goes up on his porch.

Look for: the yard and chalkboard appearing; the terminal's front page showing cash 2,500 and
loan 3,000.

## 2. Leave it alone for a week

The daily plan is on from the first day: the company buys, sells and pays its bills while you
are away. Wait or travel for 7 days (the Wait menu works; so does sleeping).

Look for: a morning report every day on the Post screen; at most one field letter a day (a
message box) and only out of combat; the weekly bills on day 7. A company left alone should end
the first week a little ahead (about 250 to 700 caps a week in the model).

## 3. Answer the post

- Memos screen: a bandit sign on the Primm road gives the odds ("our cover 45: 36 in 100 to get
  through clean, 61 with a gun"). Pick send, hire a gun for 25, or keep the wagon home.
- Left unanswered, a road memo keeps the wagon home, except on a day Primm's water is due: then
  it goes (the memo says which).
- Primm's store offers a water contract by memo: accept, counter (they may walk) or decline.

Look for: the "(default)" marker on the answer that will be taken if you say nothing.

## 4. Hire someone

Staff screen, then HIRE: 3 to 6 candidates a week. Check references before you hire; some skim.
One driver is enough; the books hold 10 people at most.

## 5. Miss a bill on purpose

The quickest way: on day 6 open the console and type `set CCCore.iCash to 0`, then wait a day.

Look for: a deferred notice that is served when you next visit the terminal or read the letter;
grace 3 days, a final notice at 5, the wagon seized at 7. Cures: pay from your own pocket, sell
the wagon, or walk away (two clicks). Three strikes within 56 days is a takeover.

## 6. Reach tier 2 (Outfit)

At tier 1 the front page's CHAINS row opens a checklist of what tier 2 needs: earned worth 1,500,
Goodsprings trust 50, Ghost Town Gunfight done, no strike in 56 days. Doing Ghost Town Gunfight
for the town gives +10 trust; each delivery gives +1.

Look for, on the tier day: a letter; an office shack next to the Prospector Saloon (its door takes
you inside: desk, terminal, map board, safe); a stall by Primm's map marker; and the Build screen
(purifier, apothecary, well rig, forager hut).

## 7. Visit Primm

The Primm branch terminal stands on the cashier counter of Vikki and Vance. It runs the same
company screens as the one in Goodsprings.
