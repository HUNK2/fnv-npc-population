# M1: One road, implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (native, chosen in the build
> workflow). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** a playable company on one road: found it with Alice's agent in Goodsprings, run one wagon
between Goodsprings and Primm from a terminal, pay the loan, grow to tier 2 or lose for reasons you
can name.

**Architecture:** two plugins from day one. `LivedInMojave.esp` with the master flag (core: shared factions, globals,
the roles kit; thin in M1) is the master of `MojaveCaravanCompany.esp` (the company). The company
is a paper simulation in quest scripts and xNVSE arrays, ticking at 06:00 with catch-up; one
terminal form with a 14-row pool shows every screen (labels rewritten, spare rows hidden by a
condition); Python model `tools/company_model.py` is the spec for every number, and an automated
in-game run must match it to the cap.

**Tech stack:** fnv-mcp (`tools/fnvmcp_call.py`, `tools/rebuild.sh`, `tools/cycle.sh`, MCP
`fnv_game launch`, `fnv_test`), xNVSE, JIP LN NVSE, JohnnyGuitar NVSE 5.28, Python 3.11 unittest.

**Spec:** `docs/design.md` (copy of the design doc, rev 150): The loops at every time scale, The
map, Starter trade, Money, Growth (tiers 1 and 2), People (hiring), The road (bandit events only),
Losing and coming back (debt ladder, strikes, busy shield), The terminal, Numbers, How it is built.
M0 facts: `docs/m0-results.md`.

## Global constraints

- Dev profile only (`C:\Modding\FNV-Dev`, profile `Dev`); test saves only (`fnvmcp_test_` prefix).
- No vanilla record or reference edited; new references in vanilla cells are added, never changed.
  Vanilla quests are read with `GetStageDone`/`GetQuestRunning`, never set.
- Terminal text: every string cut to 200 characters before `SetTerminalMenuItemText` (M0 0.8).
- Clicked row: poll flag 4 in `MenuMode 1057`, clear it, act (M0 0.5). Labels set in the
  terminal's `OnActivate` before `Activate` (M0 0.7). Opening by script: `Activate player 1`.
- Strings into plugin commands go through JIP `Console "... %q%.0f%q" args` (M0 notes).
- Script variables are only ever appended (`tools/allow_new_vars.py`); an early `return` ends the
  whole script for the frame, so each script has one block per mode or no early returns.
- Money is whole caps: every crate price is rounded half up (`floor(x + 0.5)`) at the moment it is
  booked, in the model and in the game, so they agree to the cap.
- Rolls: `roll(seed, day, key) = (day*7919 + seed*104729 + key*31) % 100`, same in both.
- Letters and message boxes only from `GameMode`, only when `player.IsInCombat == 0` (M0 0.10).
- No decompiled code, personal paths or e-mail in the repo. Commit after each passing task; push.

## My calls (M1)

- **One terminal form, virtual screens.** The design's "about 14 terminal forms" becomes one form
  `CCOfficeTerm` with a 14-row pool: every screen is a relabel plus a row count, because route C
  tells us which row was clicked and rows need no scripts of their own. Every company terminal
  (Goodsprings agent's table, Primm branch, tier 2 office) is a reference of that one form.
- **Paper staff in M1.** Hires are names and numbers on paper; real residents become hires in M2,
  when the lived-in towns pool exists (the build order already says so).
- **Primm branch terminal** sits on a desk in the Vikki and Vance casino (open from the start).
- **Goods in M1:** the six with a source on this road: purified water, healing powder, ammo, dirty
  water, herbs, building materials. Meat, stimpaks and moonshine wait for their workshops (M2+).
- **Alice's agent** is a new character, Hollis Webb, at the Prospector Saloon. If Alice is dead,
  the same agent offers the letter of credit for Jason Tagg's office (text variant only).

## Review focus

1. Sleeping or fast travelling 1 to 30 days at once: catch-up runs every day in order, at most 14
   one by one, a month folds into one summary; bills and strikes land on the right days.
2. Reloading a save mid-week: same seed, same results; terminal labels rewritten after load.
3. Cash going negative mid-day (wages before sales): the debt ladder reads only bill-day cash.
4. Clicking rows fast or on a hidden row: no double orders, no action from a hidden row.
5. Installing on an old save mid-playthrough, and with Goodsprings already lost to the Powder
   Gangers (Run Goodsprings Run done): the company still starts (landlord variant text).

### 1.8 Contracts, standing orders, hiring
- [x] One contract type: town supply contract (good, crates, interval, price, 28 days), one at a

**My calls for 1.8** (written before building it):
- *Contract offers* come as a memo (kind `contract`): Primm's general store asks for water, 4 crates
  every 3 days for 28 days, at the Primm sell price at normal stock plus 9%. Answers: accept, counter
  at +7% (a seeded roll: half the time they walk), decline (the default, due the next day). An offer
  is posted on the third day of a week when no contract runs and none was offered in the last 7 days.
- *Deliveries* ride the Goodsprings > Primm leg first (they count toward its 8 crates), loaded from
  the warehouse or bought at the Goodsprings buy price, and sold at the contract price without moving
  Primm's stock. A delivery the wagon cannot make (no wagon, kept home, no cash) is a miss: 10% of
  the delivery's value as a cost and Primm trust −3. On time: trust +1; the whole contract: +3.
- *Standing orders* change the bookkeeper's plan (so the daily plan and ACCEPT both follow them):
  keep Primm stocked with N crates of a good (carry it while Primm holds fewer and the crate does not
  lose money), sell a good only above a price, never pay more than a price for a good. They are
  edited on a slot screen reached from Orders (kind, good, and the number by steps).
- *Staff* are paper people: the founding driver and guard have names. Cover is 15 for the driver
  plus 30 for each guard, plus a fifth of each guard's skill above 50. A second guard is the lasting
  answer to bandit sign (a hired gun is one trip). Candidates (3 to 6) are posted on day 1 of each
  week, seeded: a name from a list of 40, role (guard, driver or hand), skill, honesty (hidden),
  asking wage (role base + 1 per 10 skill above 50), and a rumour line. A reference check costs 10
  and shows the honesty band (low, fair, high). Staff with honesty under 30 skim 2% of the day's
  sales. Firing the driver keeps the wagon home until a new driver is hired.
