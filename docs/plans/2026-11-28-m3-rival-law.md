# M3: Rival and law, implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (native, chosen in the build
> workflow). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the Mojave pushes back. The Crimson Caravan sells into the same towns and answers your
growth with price wars and counter-bids; the NCR charges a licence and duty, posts supply contracts
and inspects the books; a dodgy company can hide sales and run sealed crates, at the price of heat,
warrants and raids; and tier 4 (Trading house) opens.

**Architecture:** the same as M1 and M2. `tools/company_model.py` is the spec for every number (new
code in `tools/rival_model.py` and `tools/law_model.py`, called from `Company.tick`); the game follows
it in new functions (`CCRivalFn`, `CCLawFn`, `CCHeatFn`) called from `CCCoreScript`, which stays
under 14 KB compiled. New screens go in a new draw function (`CCTermDraw3`).

**Spec:** `docs/design.md`: The Crimson Caravan, The NCR, the law and the dodgy side, The road
(insurance), The late game (forward contracts), Growth (tier 4).

## Global constraints

The M1 and M2 global constraints stand. Plus, learned in M2: `Ar_List` takes at most 20 items;
`ar_Append x ""` fails at run time (use `" "`); a legacy `if` with brackets needs `eval`; `sA` and
other command names cannot be variable names; `sv_ToUpper` returns the string.

## My calls (M3)

Numbers live in `tools/law_model.py`; this is what they mean.

- **Everything new starts at tier 3** (the Caravan company), so every M1 and M2 number and in-game
  check stays as it is below it. The licence and duty were already in M1's weekly bills.
- **The rival in the ledger only.** No Crimson stock is added to the towns (it would move every M1
  price); she acts through moves. Aggression 0 to 100, 20 at the start; each week (bill day) it
  rises 10 when your best town share (your crates, contracts included, over 7 days of that town's
  use) is over 25% and above last week's, rises 5 for each NCR bid you win, and falls 5 when your
  best share is under 5%. From aggression 30 she makes one move a week at most: a price war in your
  top town when its share is over 25% (your sell prices there 15% lower for 5 days, 20% if you carry
  her Crimson Cover, since she reads your manifests), otherwise a poach warning for your
  lowest-loyalty hire under 40 (they leave the next day unless their loyalty is back to 40). Signed
  contracts keep their price. Sabotage, tip-offs, the buyout offer, grudges and the merger are M4.
- **The NCR:** the licence is 150 a season at tiers 1 and 2, 400 at tier 3, 900 at tier 4 (an unpaid
  one is arrears, as in M1, not held wagons); duty on the declared share of sales; a 20-cap toll for
  each Outpost-road trip (free at tier 4, a preferred carrier); NCR standing 0 to 100, 30 at the start,
  +1 a week of bills paid, +2 an NCR delivery, -5 a fault; inspections 30 days after tier 3 and then
  every 30 days (10 at heat 20+): hidden sales since the last one or unpaid arrears are a fault (the
  first a warning, then a fine of 5% of the last 7 days' sales). One NCR offer a week (day 5 of the
  week) while the Outpost wagon runs, heat is under 60 and the company is not an outlaw: 4 crates of
  ammunition for the Outpost every 3 days for 14 days, as a memo: bid 1.3 times market, bid 1.2, or
  pass (the default). The Crimson Caravan bids 1.25 once her aggression is 40, so then only the low
  bid wins. Payment is on delivery (the 10-day delay is M4).
- **The back room (steps 1 and 2 of the slope)**, three rows on the Towns screen from tier 3:
  declare 100, 90 or 80% of sales (heat +2 a week per 10%), sealed crates on road 1 (50 caps a run, at
  most every 3 days, heat +5, a 10% search: a 100-cap fine and heat +10) and Crimson Cover. The
  contact you meet in person moves to M4 with the other real-world encounters: in M3 the rows open
  at tier 3.
- **Heat** 0 to 100, on the front page's TOWNS row from tier 3 (the front page is full). It falls 1 a
  day without gain, 2 after 7 clean days; there is no daily cap (with the design's +3 cap and its fall
  of 2 a day, heat could never build). At 60 NCR offers freeze; at 80 (after the day's fall) a warrant
  is served as a memo and a letter, never within 7 days of a raid: pay a fixer 300 (heat back to 50,
  no raid) or let them come (the default). On its due day, 7 days later, the raid runs on paper:
  cover 60 or more wins (the company is an outlaw: standing 0, no NCR offers, no tier 4), less loses
  a fifth of the cash. Either way heat goes to 50. A second raid within 28 days revokes the licence:
  the company is closed. A raid at the office with Rangers walking in is M4.
- **Insurance:** Crimson Cover only, a toggle: 40 caps a running wagon a week with the bills, and 60%
  of a road loss paid back the same day. The reserve fund, NCR war-risk and Omerta assurance are M4.
- **Forward contracts move to M4** (they need the planner to reserve cargo, as the late game does).
- **Tier 4 (Trading house):** earned worth 40,000 (balance: the good player reaches it on day 57),
  the loan repaid, no arrears, NCR standing 50, an NCR contract delivered, not an outlaw, no strike in
  56 days. The design's "delivered in person" becomes "delivered".
- **Road crews** stay on paper: M4.
