# M4: The ending, implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (native, chosen in the build
> workflow). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the late game has stakes and an end. From tier 3 the company can bet on forward contracts;
at tier 4 it can take a patron's money and strings, and lend to small outfits as Alice once lent to
it; tier 5 (the Mojave concern) opens by beating Alice with three share blocks or by keeping a patron;
and a run ends on a "20 years on" page written from the ledger.

**Architecture:** as in M3. `tools/late_model.py` is a mixin for `Company` (the spec for every
number), called from `Company.tick`; `tools/ending_model.py` builds the closing pages from tags. In
the game the state is a quest of its own (`CCLate`, script `CCLateScript`), the rules are `CCLateFn`
(acts for the tick's steps), the texts and screens `CCLateTextFn`, and the closing pages
`CCEndingFn`. `CCCoreScript` gains only call lines.

**Spec:** `docs/design.md`: The late game (forward contracts, patrons, lending, laws, ending the
rivalry, the ending), Growth (tier 5), The Crimson Caravan (the end of the rivalry), The NCR (the
regimes).

## Global constraints

The M1 to M3 constraints stand. Learned in M3: a variable cannot share a name with a command or its
short form (`fW` is ForceWeather, `sW` is taken too); a new quest's variables can be named directly
(`CCLate.aX`) only after a build that holds its compiled script is loaded, so new records take two
builds; a function script that gains variables needs `tools/allow_new_vars.py`.

## My calls (M4)

- **Forward contracts (tier 3+).** One offer a week, on day 2 of the week, as a memo (kind 6): a town
  on a running road wants 12 crates of a market good it uses, delivered by 10 days from now, at
  today's sell price there (normal stock). Accept (the default is decline) or accept with an advance:
  half the ticket's value now, repaid out of the deliveries. Crates the wagons carry into that town
  of that good go to the ticket first (paid at its price, not sold to the market) until it is full.
  Changed while building: at today's price no road paid for a ticket's crates and the balance run missed
  almost every ticket, so a ticket pays 10% over the normal sell price, is only offered for a good that
  a running road's other end sells for less, and the bookkeeper's plan loads a ticket's crates first.
  Full: the rest of the value is paid, trust +3. Missed on the due day: 25% of the unfilled value,
  trust -5, and the advance not yet earned is owed back as arrears. At most 3 forward tickets open;
  never a hidden date (the Contracts screen lists each with its count and due day). Alice buying a
  failing ticket at 70% is left out (it needs a forecast of the fill).
- **The NCR quartermaster (tier 4, NCR standing 60).** A one-time memo (kind 7) offers preferred
  carrier status and 10,000 caps at 2% a week (interest with the bills, repaid 500 a week). The
  string: heat under 20 and not an outlaw, checked each bill day. Broken: status withdrawn, the rest
  of his loan is due as arrears in one go, NCR standing -30. One patron only; the Omertas and Mr. House
  are left for later (they need encounters and the main quest).
- **Lending (tier 4).** Every 14 days an outfit from the lived-in towns asks for a loan (memo kind 8):
  1,000, 2,000 or 3,000 (a roll), repaid over 8 weeks; the answer sets the weekly rate: 2% (gentle),
  4% (fair) or 8% (harsh), or refuse (the default). Each bill day an outfit pays its instalment
  unless its profit roll misses: the chance of a miss is 10%, 20% or 35% by the rate. Two misses in a
  row and it fails: a memo (kind 9) to seize its wagon (you recover up to the principal still owed,
  half of its value, and that town's trust -10) or forgive (trust +10 in its town). At most 2 loans
  out. Alice notices: aggression +5 when you first lend.
- **Tier 5 (the Mojave concern):** earned worth 80,000; you lead the Crimson Caravan in 3 of the 4
  towns (your weekly share there is above her fixed share: 30% in Primm and the Outpost, 10% in
  Goodsprings and Sloan; changed while building to 12% in Primm and 15% at the Outpost: at 30% no
  run ever led there); and either all three share blocks or the quartermaster's patronage kept for
  28 days.
- **Ending the rivalry: three share blocks**, bought one at a time on the Towns screen's SHARES row when its deed is
  done: lead her in Primm for 14 days in a row and pay 5,000; win an NCR bid while her aggression is
  40 or more and pay 10,000; and pay 15,000 once two blocks are held (her best captain, bought out on
  paper). Holding all three, her board votes her out: aggression 0, no more rival moves, and the end
  page says so.
- **Laws (tier 5):** favour +1 for each NCR delivery; once a season (28 days) a law can be passed for
  5 favour on the Towns screen's LAWS row, in this order: Free trade act (the NCR duty is halved),
  Carrier act (no licence fee), Cap press audit (aggression -20 at once). Changed while building: the
  Water ration act needs the good inside the price function, which the game's price function does not
  get, and an Open roads act does nothing at tier 5 (a trading house pays no toll), so both laws live
  in the bill now. The laws passed are a line in the ending. The other laws are left for later.
- **The regimes:** the mod reads which side the player took at the Dam from vanilla quest stages
  (NCR, House, Legion, Independent) only for the ending's text; the rules do not change in M4.
- **The successor chapter** is cut to the ending: a closed company (receivership, takeover, revoked)
  also gets its "20 years on" page, written as a failure.
- **The ending:** at tier 5 the Settings screen gets a `RETIRE` row (with a confirm), and when the
  company closes the page is written at once. Three pages from tags (about 60 short lines in M4: the
  towns, the roads, the people, Alice, the law, the patron and the loans, the side taken): each tag
  picks one of 2 to 4 lines by the seed. They are shown on the Post screen as the last letters, and
  the company is then closed (`iEndReason 5 retired`).

## Review focus

1. A forward ticket's crates are not also sold to the market (no double pay).
2. A missed ticket with an advance leaves arrears equal to the advance not yet earned.
3. A failed outfit's recovery never exceeds the principal still owed.
4. Breaking the quartermaster's string with his loan half repaid makes only the rest arrears.
5. Retiring writes three pages once and closes the company; a load after that keeps them.
